Privacy for Route Intent Planner

Effective 6 September 2026

What stays in your browser

Your current route, saved routes, router results, and license token stay in browser storage.

GPX and JSON files are read in your browser and exported directly to you.

What leaves your browser

The planner makes no automatic route, map, analytics, advertising, or tile requests.

When you choose Optimize gaps, each open gap’s two endpoints go to the public bicycle router at routing.openstreetmap.de.

Locked segments and saved routes are not sent.

License purchase or verification sends your license token to the Sociobot billing API.

Sociobot/Dodo processes checkout as merchant of record.

Offline storage and deletion

The service worker stores the app files and artwork for offline use.

Cached routed gaps remain exportable offline. New gap routing needs a connection.

Delete saved routes in the planner. Clear this site’s browser data to remove all local data.

Downloaded GPX and JSON files remain where you saved them.

Privacy questions

Open a request in the public product repository (external).

Material policy changes will update the effective date on this page.

Route Intent Planner stores routes in your browser and sends only selected gap endpoints for routing.
