Advisory planning only

Route Intent Planner preserves coordinates you provide. It does not verify road access, legal restrictions, closures, traffic, surface quality, weather, or personal fitness. When you explicitly choose Optimize gaps, only that open section is shaped on an OpenStreetMap-compatible bicycle network; authored endpoints and every locked corridor remain unchanged. Inspect every route with current local information before riding.

Your content

You retain ownership of routes you create or import and are responsible for having the right to use and share them. The software is provided under its repository license without a warranty of route safety or availability.

Route Tape purchase

Route Tape is not currently offered for purchase, so the planner does not show a checkout link. When the factory enables its one-time US$9 license, it will unlock more than three saved route tapes plus JSON archive backup and restore. Core GPX import/export and safety warnings remain free. Sociobot/Dodo will be merchant of record; checkout, tax, receipts, and refunds will be handled there.

Acceptable use and availability

Do not misuse the billing verification service or use the product to facilitate unlawful access. We may fix, improve, or discontinue hosted availability, but exported GPX and JSON remain standard files under your control.
